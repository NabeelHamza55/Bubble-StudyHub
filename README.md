# Bubble Certification Study Hub

**Bubble Study Hub** is a free study companion for the [Bubble Developer Certification](https://bubble.io/certification). It brings practice questions, flashcards, and curated learning links into one place so you can prepare for the exam without signing up or paying for a course.

Your progress saves automatically in your browser — pick up where you left off anytime.

**Live app:** [https://nabeelhamza55.github.io/Bubble-Certification/](https://nabeelhamza55.github.io/Bubble-Certification/)

---

## About the app

The real Bubble certification exam is tough: **90 questions**, **3.5 hours**, and you need **80%** to pass. A large share of questions are **multi-answer** (select every correct option), and many certified developers say **Performance & Workload** is the topic they were least prepared for.

Bubble Study Hub mirrors that reality. You get cert-style practice, manual-based scenarios, and extra drills focused on the hardest question types — plus flashcards and official-topic resources so you are not hunting links across the web.

**Important:** This app is **not affiliated with Bubble.io**. All questions are original practice material aligned with the public syllabus, not leaked official exam items.

---

## What you can study

### Practice Exam (50 questions)

A mock exam mix of scenarios, multiple choice, multi-answer, and true/false — with explanations after you submit so you learn from mistakes.

### Doc & Scenario Quiz (100 questions)

Manual-based questions with real-world builder scenarios. Use this to validate that you understand the documentation before sitting the full mock exam.

### Hard Mode (35 questions)

Multi-answer only, written to feel like the trickiest exam questions. You must select **every** correct option; miss one and the question is wrong. Aim for **85%+** here before your timed practice exam.

### Flashcards

Seven topic areas matching the certification syllabus. Reveal answers at your own pace; the app remembers which cards you have already opened.

### Study Material

Docs, videos, and blog posts organized by official topic — a structured starting point when you do not know where to read next.

### Resources

Extra community links, mock tests, expert advice, and workload concepts worth memorizing before test day.

---

## Recommended study path

1. Skim **Study Material** and note weak areas (start with **Workload** if you are unsure).  
2. Run **Flashcards** until you can answer without peeking.  
3. Complete the **Doc & Scenario Quiz** (100 questions).  
4. Push through **Hard Mode** until you are consistently above 85%.  
5. Take the **Practice Exam** under timed conditions; target **80%+**.  
6. Review wrong answers and revisit the manual sections they point to.
